# Calo Fit — Known Limitations & Setup Requirements

## Known Limitations
1. **Camera Recognition Approximations:** A photograph cannot determine exact oil volume, internal cooked fats, or gram weights. The AI scanner provides an initial nutritional estimate and strictly requires user confirmation and portion verification prior to logging.
2. **Medical Context:** Calo Fit is designed for healthy lifestyle and nutritional awareness; it is not a clinical medical device.
3. **Billing Integration:** Google Play Billing provider interfaces are structured; purchases remain disabled until production merchant keys and billing licenses are configured.

## Setup Requirements
- Target: Android 7.0 (API 24) or higher.
- Compiled with Android SDK 36 (Android 15+).
- Kotlin 2.2+ with Jetpack Compose.
